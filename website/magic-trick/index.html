Install

  1. Back up your SD card. Power on your Chromatic and connect a USB data cable.
  2. Extract the download and open Magic Trick Installer. Close any other device apps.
  3. Select your Chromatic and a backup folder. Keep power and USB connected until both firmware components are verified.

Before you start

The app checks your device and makes a private MCU backup. It does not back up your SD card or FPGA.

Windows may need MRUpdater USB drivers. Linux may need USB permissions. Unsupported firmware or layouts are rejected before writing.
