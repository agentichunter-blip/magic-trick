Meet your new installer.

Choose your computer. The desktop app checks compatibility, creates and verifies a private MCU backup, and installs the art31 application. Python is included—you don’t need to install it separately.

Already running ChroMagic?
Requires ChroMagic 1.0.1 or a compatible art-series MCU 0.13.4, FPGA 18.38, and the firmware-version console command. Stock devices and older firmware without that command are not supported. Start with upstream ChroMagician.

Installer preview 0.1.0 · Firmware art31 · Free download

Preview limitations: These packages are unsigned and not Apple-notarized. Hardware flashing through this installer has not yet been tested end to end. Automated tests do not establish compatibility with every USB driver or device. Device Status continues to show the original art31 version names.

Three steps. One small transformation.

01

Prepare

Back up your SD card. Power on your Chromatic, connect a data-capable USB cable, and close other device apps.

02

Install

Extract the download, open Magic Trick Installer, select your device and choose a private backup folder. Keep power and USB connected.

03

Make it yours

Add your own games and authorized artwork or previews. Nothing from your game library is bundled or uploaded.
